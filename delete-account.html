Official Policy

Account & Data Deletion Request

Application: Checklist Plus | Developer: Monoframe

1. How to Request Account Deletion

Users have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data at any time.

Option A. Direct in-app deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the Checklist Plus app.
  2. Navigate to the drawer menu or Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm to permanently wipe all data immediately.

Option B. Remote deletion via Email (If app is uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can submit an account deletion request via email. Please include the registered email address of your account. Our support team will process the permanent deletion within 3 business days.

Support Contact: support@mono-frame.com

Send Deletion Request Email

2. Data Types Deleted

  • Account Credentials & Profile: Email address, nickname, and profile image.
  • Application Content: All groups, projects, tasks, and completion checklists created by the user.
  • Collaboration Records: Project member invitations and team linkage data.

3. Retention and Deletion Policy

Upon receiving and confirming the deletion request, all account credentials and personal data are permanently and irreversibly purged from our production databases and cloud servers. Transaction records related to in-app purchases may be retained strictly as required by applicable tax or commerce laws.
